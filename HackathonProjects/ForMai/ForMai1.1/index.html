How ForMai Works

Click a Field

Click on any input field on any webpage. ForMai detects it automatically — no setup needed.

2

AI Analyzes It

Google Gemini reads the field's label, name, and context to understand exactly what's being asked.

3

Features

Click any input and get a structured breakdown: what's being asked, why, expected format, tips, and common mistakes — in under 120 words.

FAQ

Is ForMai free to use?
Yes — ForMai is completely free. There is no subscription, no account required, and no hidden charges. It uses your browser's session to communicate with the Gemini AI API.
Does ForMai store my personal information?
No. ForMai never stores, logs, or transmits your personal information. It only sends the form field's label, placeholder, name attribute, and type to the AI — never anything you type. All suggestions exist only for the current browser session.
How does ForMai explain form fields?
When you click on a form field, ForMai reads its technical metadata (label, placeholder, name, type) and sends it to Google Gemini AI. Gemini returns a short, structured explanation covering: what the field asks, why it's needed, the expected format, helpful tips, and common mistakes to avoid.
Can ForMai fill forms automatically?
No, and this is intentional. ForMai only explains — it never fills, pre-fills, or submits any form field. You remain fully in control of every answer you provide. This design keeps ForMai safe and ethical.
What is Snip Mode?
Snip Mode lets you draw a selection box over any area of a webpage. ForMai captures that region as an image and sends it to Google Gemini's vision AI, which analyzes all visible form fields at once and explains each one — ideal for complex multi-field forms.
Does it work on all websites?
ForMai works on virtually all websites that have HTML input fields or textareas — including dynamic single-page apps built with React, Angular, or Vue. Some browser-internal pages (like chrome:// URLs) cannot be accessed by extensions due to browser security rules.
